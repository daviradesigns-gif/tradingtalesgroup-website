
## Style Decisions

The site now treats **the story behind the trade** as the core visual motif. Language and directional cues emphasize field notes, annotated lessons, decision-making, and perspective rather than generic market-growth imagery.

The signature green is reserved as a **signal color** for decisive emphasis, key labels, directional actions, and selected words. Charcoal and warm off-white carry the main editorial atmosphere, while the community image is intentionally subdued with a grid-like editorial overlay.

The copy voice is intentionally calm, disciplined, and process-led. Phrases such as “Process over promise” and “Perspective over noise” replace generic wealth or lifestyle language.
